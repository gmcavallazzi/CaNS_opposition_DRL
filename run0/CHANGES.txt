WHAT THIS FIXES
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Bang-bang control behavior
  - Policy too reactive to instantaneous observations
  - No memory of what it just did
  - Creates high-frequency oscillations

SOLUTION: Add prev_action to observations
  - Policy sees: [u, w, prev_action]
  - Can learn: "I just did X, so I should keep doing X or adjust smoothly"
  - Natural smooth control without temporal loss on old policy actions
